# RFQ Bid Sheet — Standard Template

The standardized form every vendor receives in a consortium RFQ round. Standardization is the point: identical questions to all bidders, scored identically, so awards are defensible to members, auditors, and the bank (the E&I lesson from the [precedents page]({{ '/case-studies' | relative_url }}) — documented arm's-length dealing is legal protection).

| # | Item | Vendor response |
|---|---|---|
| A1 | Legal entity name / state / years operating | |

| A3 | DEA-registered third-party lab used for COAs? Name + last audit date | |
| A4 | Product liability insurance: carrier + per-occurrence limit (min $1M required) | |
| A5 | Sample COA attached per product grade bid? (delta-9 ≤0.3% dry weight + pesticide/heavy-metal/microbial panels — batch-specific, not generic) | |
| A6 | Written batch-failure recourse policy attached? | |

*Any fail in Part A disqualifies the bid regardless of price. This is the vetting service members pay for.*

## Scoring & Award Rules

1. **Part A:** pass/fail. Failures documented in writing to the vendor.
2. **Part B → total landed cost model:** price after tier discounts, adjusted for payment-term value and freight. The formula lives with the Product Committee; the *inputs* from this sheet go into it unmodified.
3. **Part C weighted ~20%** of total score — quality/service can't win against a bad price but can't be ignored at equal price.
4. **Award memo:** one page per category — winner, scores, rationale — retained permanently (bank artifact, member-transparency artifact, antitrust artifact).
5. **Loser summary:** aggregate score ranges published to all bidders. No individual competitor's pricing disclosed.

## Cover Email Frame (for the negotiation lead)

> [Vendor] — [Consortium] is requesting bids for supply of THCA hemp flower to a committed purchasing group of [N] independent retailers in PA/NJ representing $[X]/month in documented category spend (baseline invoices verified). Please complete the attached bid sheet in full by [date]. Awards are made by committee on total landed cost and documented service capability. Bidders receive an aggregate outcome summary. Questions in writing only, answered to all bidders simultaneously.
