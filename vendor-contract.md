# Vendor Supply Contract — Working Skeleton

The consortium-side contract: what [Consortium Entity] signs with each awarded distributor/processor. This is the document that converts aggregated demand into enforceable pricing — the exact thing a spreadsheet group never has. Every bracket is a negotiation variable; **[COUNSEL]** marks mandatory attorney review.

---

## Article I — Parties & Definitions

- **Organization** — the consortium entity ([form per Entity Structure]({{ '/entity-structure' | relative_url }}))
- **Vendor** — licensed hemp processor/distributor
- **Covered Products** — SKUs/grade bands per Schedule 1 (mirrors winning bid sheet)
- **Contract Price** — pricing per Schedule 2, inclusive of any volume tiers and retroactive ladders
- **Member Account** — an ordering account opened for each Member under Vendor's system with Contract Price loaded (**deviated pricing** — see [mechanisms]({{ '/case-studies' | relative_url }}))
- **Purchase Report** — monthly line-item data feed per Article VI

## Article II — Term & Volume

1. Initial term: **6 months** (year-one standard; market reprices faster than annual terms)
2. Renewal: mutual written agreement; no automatic rollover (prevents stale pricing)
3. **Volume expectations, not guarantees:** Organization represents expected aggregate purchases of $[X]/month based on documented member demand; no purchase obligation by Organization itself — members order directly. *(Critical structural point: the consortium never holds inventory or takes title.)*
4. If aggregate purchases fall below $[X]% of representation for two consecutive quarters, either party may terminate on 30 days notice — protects the vendor from phantom volume and keeps us honest about our own recruiting.

## Article III — Pricing & Enforcement

1. Vendor shall load Contract Price on every Member Account before contract effective date
2. Price increases require 60-day written notice AND are capped at contract expiry unless mutually amended — no mid-term repricing
3. **Most-favored-nation lite:** Vendor warrants Contract Price ≤ price offered to any comparable wholesale account of similar aggregate volume. *(Full MFN clauses often get negotiated out; a "comparable account" formulation survives better.)*
4. Retroactive tier credit per Schedule 2 §B3: if aggregate purchases cross thresholds, Vendor issues credit memos within 30 days of quarter close
5. Administrative fee: Organization receives [0–3]% of purchases under this contract, invoiced [monthly/quarterly], disclosed to members per the transparency covenant

## Article IV — Product Quality & Compliance

1. Every batch shipped under a Member Account carries a batch-specific COA from a DEA-registered lab: delta-9 THC ≤0.3% dry weight + full pesticide/heavy-metal/microbial panels
2. COAs delivered electronically at shipment; retained by Vendor ≥[2] years; available to Organization on request within 5 business days
3. **Batch failure recourse:** member (or Organization) may reject non-conforming product; Vendor replaces or credits within [15] days including inbound freight
4. Regulatory change clause: if Covered Products become unlawful in a member's state, affected orders void without penalty; neither party liable for compliance-driven order changes
5. Vendor indemnifies Organization and Members against claims arising from non-compliant product; insurance certificate per RFQ Part A maintained throughout term

## Article V — Ordering & Fulfillment

1. Members order directly through Vendor's normal channels against their Member Account
2. Fill-rate guarantee: ≥[95]% of ordered lines in-stock or substitute offered at equal-or-better grade; chronic shorting (<90% two consecutive months) is a termination event
3. Lead time target [X] days; delivery terms per bid (freight allowance threshold)
4. Allocation policy for scarce product: pro-rata across Member Accounts by trailing-quarter purchase share — **no vendor discretion, no insider favoritism**

## Article VI — Data & Audit

1. Vendor delivers Purchase Report by the 5th of each month: member ID, date, SKU, qty, unit price actually charged
2. Organization audit right: sample invoices quarterly; discrepancies above contract price → credit memo within 15 days plus [2]% penalty credit *(the penalty makes quiet repricing unprofitable rather than merely detectable)*
3. Vendor may use aggregate, de-identified volume data publicly only with Organization's written approval

## Article VII — Assignment & Change of Entity

**[COUNSEL]** but standard: either party may assign to a successor entity (our planned [501(c)(6) conversion]({{ '/entity-structure' | relative_url }})) with written notice; assignment to unrelated third parties requires consent. This clause costs nothing now and saves re-signing every vendor later.

## Article VIII — Antitrust Independence

**[COUNSEL]** Both parties acknowledge: Organization negotiates independently for its members; Vendor sets its own prices without member-to-member coordination; nothing herein constitutes resale price maintenance (Members set their own retail prices freely).

## Article IX — Termination & Wind-Down

1. Termination for cause: material breach uncured after 15 days' notice
2. Immediate termination: licensure loss, failed audit with fraud indicators, insurance lapse
3. Effect: Member Accounts remain active through a 30-day wind-down at Contract Price; accrued tier credits paid within 45 days; Purchase Reports delivered through final day

## Schedules

- **Schedule 1:** Covered Products / grade bands / SKU list
- **Schedule 2:** Contract Price sheet incl. tiers, ladder mechanics, admin fee %
- **Schedule 3:** Compliance documentation requirements (COA spec, licensure copies)

## Negotiation Notes for the Team

- **Trade concessions deliberately:** vendors will resist MFN-lite and the audit penalty hardest. Priority ranking: (1) deviated pricing load, (2) monthly Purchase Report — these two make enforcement work; everything else is negotiable currency.
- **The 6-month term is our discipline,** not just market caution: it forces an annual rhythm of competitive tension (two RFQ rounds/year), which is what keeps renewal pricing honest.
- **Never sign exclusivity** (RFQ B10 carries over here): multi-vendor awards per category are our single-distributor-failure hedge.
