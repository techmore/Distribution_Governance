# Why Vendors Pay to Participate

This page speaks directly to distributors and processors considering consortium contracts — including the part that sounds backwards at first: **why would I give a buying group 2% of my sales when I can sell direct?**

---

## The Arithmetic of Your Current Book

Take a territory with 40 independent smoke shops each buying ~$2,500/month of THCA product:

| Cost center | 40 small accounts | 1 consortium account |
|---|---|---|
| Onboarding (credit checks, paperwork, setup) | 40× | 1× |
| Sales coverage | Rep visits/calls across 40 storefronts; churn means constantly re-prospecting | One relationship manager |
| Order processing | 40 order streams, 40 invoice cycles, 40 collections risks | One commercial relationship (members still ship individually) |
| Demand pattern | Erratic; each shop re-shops price monthly; churn when a competitor calls | Contracted quarterly minimums; predictable baseline you can buy inventory against |
| Marketing/trade spend | Scattered samples and deals with no visibility | One negotiated program with full volume transparency |
| Bad-debt exposure | Spread across 40 small balances nobody monitors well | Concentrated in one credit-reviewed counterparty |

The consortium isn't asking for charity on the admin fee. It's offering to consolidate your most expensive, least predictable revenue into your cheapest, most predictable revenue. **The 1–3% fee is priced against real cost removal** — and every healthcare GPO over a century has proven vendors net out ahead on that trade, which is why the model still exists.

## What You Get Concretely

- **Committed aggregate volume.** Members sign quarterly minimums ([membership structure]({{ '/consortium-model' | relative_url }})). You're not hoping the volume shows up — it's documented, baselined from real invoices before we ever contacted you.
- **One negotiation per term instead of forty standing discounts.** Your pricing integrity lives in one contract rather than leaking through forty informal arrangements.
- **Deviated pricing infrastructure does the discounting for you.** Contract prices load on member accounts automatically ([mechanism detail]({{ '/case-studies' | relative_url }})); your reps stop fielding individual haggle calls.
- **Clean compliance posture by design.** Our vendor qualification gate requires DEA-registered-lab COAs, licensure verification, insurance certificates ([RFQ Part A]({{ '/rfq-bid-sheet' | relative_url }})). Winning our award is third-party validation you can show regulators, banks, and larger retail partners.
- **Growth without prospecting cost.** Every new member shop joins *your* account. Recruitment — historically the most expensive function in distribution — becomes someone else's job.

## What We Ask, Plainly

- **Real tier pricing** against documented committed volume — not street-minus-a-token
- **Deviated price loading + monthly line-item purchase reports** — these two are non-negotiable because they're the enforcement mechanism protecting *you* from rivals undercutting the contract and *us* from quiet repricing ([contract Art. III & VI]({{ '/vendor-contract' | relative_url }}))
- **Allocation by formula during shortages** — pro-rata across member accounts, no discretion; protects everyone including you from favoritism claims
- **A 6-month initial term**, renewed only on performance — short terms protect both sides in this market

## What We Don't Ask

- No exclusivity — we award multiple vendors per category deliberately, and you should want the competitive tension anyway
- No inventory risk for us — members order and receive directly; the consortium never takes title
- No resale-price control — members set their own retail prices freely (antitrust independence is contractual, [Art. VIII]({{ '/vendor-contract' | relative_url }}))
- No access to individual member data beyond what the purchase report contains

## The Honest Caveats

Vendors deserve the same straight talk members get:

- **You will make less margin per unit.** The bet is volume × predictability beats margin × churn. If your cost-to-serve on small accounts is already near zero (e.g., they all come through your existing inside-sales motion), that bet may not pay — say so in the RFQ; fee percentage negotiates.
- **Enforcement cuts both ways.** The audit penalty for above-contract invoicing ([Art. VI]({{ '/vendor-contract' | relative_url }})) applies from day one. If your organization can't guarantee price-sheet discipline across its own staff, fix that before bidding.
- **We are new.** Five shops today, targeting thirty within a year. Early awards are pilot-sized; you're underwriting growth potential, not current scale — which is exactly why early partners get locked-in advantages (retroactive tier ladders mean you benefit as we grow, instead of being renegotiated against).

## How to Participate

Watch for RFQ rounds in your category or contact the consortium directly. Bids are standardized ([bid sheet]({{ '/rfq-bid-sheet' | relative_url }})), scored by committee, awarded on total landed cost plus service capability, with written rationale retained and an aggregate outcome summary sent to all bidders. Losing bidders stay in the file for the next round — most categories run multi-vendor.
