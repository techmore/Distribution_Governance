# PA/NJ Resale Compliance Matrix

**⚠ Read the alert box first — it changes the project's risk picture.**

<div class="disclaimer">
  <strong>Material finding (Aug 2026):</strong> Team direction was to ignore <em>upcoming</em> regulatory changes. But research shows New Jersey's ban on THCA flower and most intoxicating hemp products is <strong>already in force</strong> (since April 13, 2026), and a federal redefinition of hemp (H.R. 5371 §781, signed Nov 2025) takes effect <strong>November 12, 2026</strong>, measuring total THC including THCA — under which virtually all current THCA retail products become federally non-compliant. This isn't a hypothetical future change; it's the operating environment. See "Strategic Implications" below.
</div>

## Federal Layer

| Item | Status |
|---|---|
| 2018 Farm Bill definition | Hemp = ≤0.3% delta-9 THC dry weight. This is the framework the consortium was scoped against |
| H.R. 5371 §781 (signed Nov 12, 2025) | Redefines hemp at ≤0.3% **total THC** (delta-9 + THCA×0.877 + delta-8/10, HHC, etc.) and caps finished containers at **0.4mg total THC**. Effective **Nov 12, 2026** |
| Effect on THCA flower | A 25% THCA flower tests ~22% total THC → cannabis under the new definition. Non-compliant federally after Nov 12, 2026 |
| Synthetics | DEA position: synthetic THC isomers never qualified as hemp; NJ bans synthesized cannabinoids outright |

## New Jersey (as of Aug 2026)

| Product category | Status | Who may sell |
|---|---|---|
| THCA flower / >0.3% total THC hemp | ❌ **Banned since Apr 13, 2026** (P.L.2025 c.215, amended P.L.2026 c.7) | CRC-licensed dispensaries only (as cannabis) |
| Synthesized cannabinoids (delta-8 from CBD, HHC) | ❌ Banned since Jan 13, 2026 | Nobody |
| Hemp edibles >0.4mg total THC/container | ❌ Banned since Apr 13, 2026 | CRC-licensed only |
| Intoxicating hemp beverages | ⚠️ Wind-down: ABC liquor licensees + Class 5 dispensaries until **Nov 13, 2026**; ≤5mg/serving, ≤10mg/container; then CRC-only | Narrowing to zero for smoke shops |
| Online sales of hemp-derived products | ❌ Banned since Jan 13, 2026 | — |
| Age floor | 21+ for any detectable THC product | All retailers |
| Compliant CBD/CBG products (≤0.3% total THC) | ✅ Legal | Licensed hemp retailers |

Sources: [NJ-CRC intoxicating hemp FAQ](https://www.nj.gov/cannabis/resources/faqs/intoxicating-hemp/) (official), S4509 analyses.

## Strategic Implications — Decision Required

The founding scope ("farm-bill loophole, ignore upcoming changes") assumed both states were open. Reality:

1. **NJ is closed for the core categories now.** Recruiting NJ smoke shops into a THCA purchasing consortium would be organizing purchases their members cannot lawfully resell. NJ participation must be limited to compliant categories (CBD/CBG lines, accessories) or deferred entirely.

2. **The federal clock runs out Nov 12, 2026** (~2.5 months from this writing). After that date, the consortium's core category is federally non-compliant regardless of state law — unless enforcement posture or implementing regulations soften the blow, neither of which should be bet on.

3. **Options for the team:**
   - **A. Accelerate:** compress the roadmap ([banking]({{ '/banking-operations' | relative_url }}) → seed members → pilot RFQ) into a PA-only operation that proves the model before November. The consortium machinery itself (governance, negotiation, reconciliation) survives any product-category shift.
   - **B. Pivot the category:** build the consortium around compliant-forever adjacent categories — glass, packaging, POS, compliant CBD lines, eventually licensed-cannabis-adjacent services if partners pursue licenses. Weaker price-aggregation story; stronger longevity story.
   - **C. Hybrid (recommended for discussion):** launch PA-only, flower-pilot immediately per the existing roadmap, with the wind-down provisions ([agreement Art X]({{ '/membership-agreement' | relative_url }})) treated as live planning rather than boilerplate — because they will likely actually be exercised.

4. **What does NOT change:** every structural decision on this site — governance, operator neutrality, entity form, banking approach, member agreements — is category-independent. The consortium framework is the durable asset; THCA flower is the launch vehicle.
