# Letter of Intent — Member Recruiting Template

A one-page LOI for Phase 2 seed recruiting. Purpose: convert conversations into documented committed demand (≥$25K/month aggregate) *before* the first RFQ goes out — distributors negotiate against paper, not enthusiasm. This is deliberately non-binding on savings; it binds only seriousness.

---

## Template

> **NON-BINDING LETTER OF INTENT TO JOIN PURCHASING CONSORTIUM**
>
> **Business:** ______________________  **DBA/Location:** ____________
> **Owner/Officer:** ______________  **Contact:** ____________________
>
> **1. Intent.** [Consortium Entity], a purchasing organization under formation, intends to negotiate supply contracts for THCA hemp products on behalf of its members. The undersigned business ("Prospective Member") intends to become a member upon the consortium's launch, subject to final membership terms.
>
> **2. Current category spend.** Prospective Member currently purchases approximately $________ per month in THCA hemp products (flower / pre-rolls / vapes / concentrates / edibles — circle categories), based on the last [3] months of invoices, which it will make available in redacted form for baseline pricing analysis.
>
> **3. Commitment tier intended** (check one):
>
> ☐ **Member** — no volume commitment; access to negotiated contract pricing
> ☐ **Committed** — quarterly minimum purchase of $________ (recommended ≥80% of current spend); deeper tier pricing; priority allocation
> ☐ **Founding** — capital contribution of $________; board eligibility; longest rate lock
>
> **4. What this LOI is and is not.**
> - It IS: authorization to include Prospective Member's stated spend in aggregated demand presented to vendors during negotiations; agreement to complete baseline price documentation within 30 days of request; indication of serious intent governing tier planning.
> - It IS NOT: a binding purchase obligation; a membership agreement; a guarantee of any specific pricing or savings. Final terms will be set in the Membership Agreement ([skeleton here]({{ '/membership-agreement' | relative_url }})), and Prospective Member may decline to sign it at no cost.
>
> **5. Confidentiality.** Both parties keep this letter and all exchanged pricing data confidential. Individual member pricing data is never shared with other members or vendors beyond aggregate figures.
>
> **6. Term.** Valid for 180 days from signature.
>
> Signature: ____________________ Date: ________
> For the Consortium: ____________ Date: ________

## Usage Notes for the Team

- **Get the invoice baseline at LOI time, not later.** Redacted recent invoices (vendor names visible, prices visible, shop identity masked) are what make our first RFQ credible — both to vendors (real demand) and to members later (proven savings). An LOI without baseline documentation is worth half as much.
- **Push for Committed tier on the first ask.** The tier ladder exists so "Member" feels like a safe fallback. If a shop won't commit 80% of spend, that's diagnostic — they're the free-riders the [trade-offs page]({{ '/strengths-weaknesses' | relative_url }}) warns about, and their demand shouldn't anchor our vendor pitch anyway.
- **Track the aggregate weekly.** The recruiting milestone is ≥$25K/month documented committed spend (per the [roadmap]({{ '/banking-operations' | relative_url }})). At an average of $2,500/shop, that's ten Committed-tier signatures — a concrete number every partner can carry into conversations.
- **Founding capital ask:** keep it modest ($2–5K range) and tie it explicitly to covering formation costs + the first-RFQ kill-switch budget from the [financial model]({{ '/financial-model' | relative_url }}). Founding Members should know exactly what their money buys before it's spent.
